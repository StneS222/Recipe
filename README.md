# My Favorite Recipes

Here are some of my favorite recipes that I love to cook and share with friends and family.

## 1. Spicy Miso Ramen

![Spicy miso ramen with homemade broth and smoked paprika chili oil](food/smoky-spicy-vegan-ramen-with-homemade-broth-and-smoked-paprika-chili-oil-noodle-soup-thumb-1-1.jpg)

This is a delicious and hearty ramen recipe that combines the rich flavors of miso with a spicy kick. Perfect for a cozy night in!

### Ingredients:

* 4 cups of vegetable broth
* 2 tablespoons of miso paste
* 1 tablespoon of soy sauce
* 1 teaspoon of sesame oil
* 2 cloves of garlic, minced
* 1 teaspoon of ginger, grated
* 1 pack of ramen noodles
* 1 cup of sliced mushrooms
* 1 cup of chopped bok choy
* 1/2 cup of corn kernels
* 1/2 teaspoon of red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
* Chopped green onions for garnish

## 2. Veggie Kebabs

![Colorful vegetable kebabs made with bell peppers, zucchini, onions, tomatoes, and tofu are arranged on a serving plate in an outdoor grilling setting. The bright vegetables create a fresh and cheerful mood.](food/vegan-kebabs-17.jpg)

These colorful veggie kebabs are perfect for grilling season! They are packed with flavor and can be served as a main dish or a side.

### Ingredients:

* 1 red bell pepper, cut into chunks
* 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into chunks
* 1 zucchini, sliced into rounds
* 1 red onion, cut into chunks
* 1 cup of cherry tomatoes
* 1 block of grilled tofu, cubed
* 1/4 cup of olive oil
* 2 tablespoons of balsamic vinegar
* 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
* Salt and pepper to taste

## 3. Vegan Vanilla Ice Cream

![Scoops of creamy vegan vanilla ice cream sit in a dish, presented in a simple setting that creates a cool and inviting mood.](food/Vegan-Vanilla-Bean-Ice-Cream2536.jpg)

This creamy and delicious vegan vanilla ice cream is made with coconut milk and is perfect for a sweet treat on a hot day!

### Ingredients:

* 2 cans of full-fat coconut milk
* 3/4 cup of sugar
* 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract
* Pinch of salt

## 4. Tomato Egg

This comforting and classic Chinese stir-fry combines tender scrambled eggs with juicy, sweet-and-savory stewed tomatoes, making it the ultimate quick meal over steamed rice!

Ingredients:

* 4 large eggs, beaten
* 3 medium ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
* 2 stalks green onion, chopped (white and green parts separated)
* 1 tablespoon ketchup
* 1 tablespoon oyster sauce 
* 1 teaspoon sugar
* 1/2 teaspoon salt (divided)
* 2 tablespoons cooking oil
